**Project 2: PeoplePulse - Workforce Attrition and Productivity Analytics**



**Project Overview**

**Case Study:** PeoplePulse HR and business leaders need data-driven workforce planning but often rely on static monthly spreadsheets. PeoplePulse provides a dynamic people analytics command center.



As a Data Analytics Intern, you will model employee lifecycle data and generate planning insights for HR and management.



**Key Objectives \& Technical Architecture**

**Phase 1: HR Data Foundation**

* Integrate employee master, attendance, performance, and hiring data.
* Build dimensions for function, location, grade, and tenure.
* Define base KPIs: headcount, attrition, absenteeism, hiring lead time.

**Phase 2: Attrition and Risk Analytics**

* Identify high attrition cohorts by function and tenure.
* Correlate leave trends, manager changes, and exits.
* Build attrition early-warning indicators.

**Phase 3: Productivity and Hiring Insights**

* Track productivity metrics by role family.
* Measure hiring source quality and time-to-fill.
* Analyze probation and early-exit outcomes.

**Phase 4: Workforce Planning Dashboard**

* Quarterly headcount forecast model.
* Critical role risk dashboard.
* Department-level talent health scorecard.



**Prerequisites**

1. SQL and HR metrics
2. BI tools
3. Optional predictive modeling basics



**Deliverables**

1. Workforce analytics data model.
2. Attrition and productivity dashboards.
3. HR planning recommendation deck.



**Project Timeline \& Deadlines**

Week 1: Data integration and KPI baseline.

Week 2: Attrition diagnostics and segmentation.

Week 3: Productivity and hiring analytics.

Week 4: Forecasting and final reporting.
