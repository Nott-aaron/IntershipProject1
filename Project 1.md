**Project 1: CareMetrics - Hospital Operations and Patient Flow Analytics**



**Project Overview**

**Case Study:** CareMetrics Hospitals generate large operational data but struggle to convert it into service improvements. CareMetrics provides visibility into patient flow bottlenecks and capacity usage.



As a Data Analytics Intern, you will build a clinical operations analytics layer for administrators and department heads.



**Key Objectives \& Technical Architecture**

**Phase 1: Data Consolidation**

* Integrate registration, appointment, admission, and discharge data.
* Standardize department, doctor, and service dimensions.
* Define KPIs: average wait time, LOS, occupancy rate, turnaround time.

**Phase 2: Flow Bottleneck Analytics**

* Measure delays across registration, consultation, diagnostics, billing.
* Analyze OPD peak-hour congestion.
* Identify discharge process blockers.

**Phase 3: Capacity Optimization Insights**

* Bed utilization patterns by ward and specialty.
* Staffing demand indicators by time block.
* Predicted congestion alerts.

**Phase 4: Leadership Dashboard**

* Department-level KPI drill-down.
* Daily operations scorecard.
* Monthly service quality trend reports.



**Prerequisites**

1. SQL and operations analytics
2. Dashboard development
3. Basic healthcare process knowledge.



**Deliverables**

1. Unified hospital ops dataset.
2. Patient flow and capacity dashboards.
3. Actionable bottleneck analysis report.



**Project Timeline \& Deadlines**

Week 1: Data model and KPI definitions.

Week 2: Flow analysis and bottleneck detection.

Week 3: Capacity and staffing insights.

Week 4: Dashboard QA and stakeholder handoff.
